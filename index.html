Privacy policy for Doodh Khata: Milk Tracker

Updated November 15, 2026

Doodh Khata: Milk Tracker is an offline ledger for tracking daily milk deliveries and monthly bills. This policy explains what happens to information entered into the app.

Data collection and storage

The app does not collect, upload, share, or sell any personal information. All entries, including daily milk quantities, rates, currency choices, and off-day notes, are stored directly on your phone using standard Android local storage.

Accounts and internet access

The app does not use accounts or logins. It works offline and does not connect to external servers or cloud databases.

Deleting your data

Because everything stays on your phone, your entries remain as long as the app is installed. You can delete all saved records at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's Settings (Settings > Apps > Doodh Khata > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics libraries, advertising networks, or tracking tools.

Sharing bills

When you share a bill summary to WhatsApp or messages, the app opens Android's standard share menu with the text. That message is sent only when you choose to send it. The app does not send anything in the background.

Children

The app is a utility for households and milk sellers. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Policy changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear on this page with a new date.
