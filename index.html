Privacy Policy for Doodh Khata: Milk Tracker

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Doodh Khata: Milk Tracker ("the application", "we", "us") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding our data practices.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Doodh Khata: Milk Tracker is an offline utility app. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or sensitive user data with any external servers, third parties, or advertising networks.

All data entered into the application—including daily milk quantities, rates per liter, currency choices, and off-day records—is stored locally and exclusively on your device's internal storage using standard Android local preferences.

2. User Accounts and Network Transmission

The application does not require you to register, log in, or create an account. It operates entirely offline without connecting to external cloud databases.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides strictly on your local device:

  • Your data remains on your device for as long as you keep the application installed.
  • You can delete all stored records and settings at any time by clearing the application storage in your device Settings (Settings > Apps > Doodh Khata > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

4. Device Permissions

Doodh Khata: Milk Tracker does not request or require sensitive runtime device permissions. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or phone storage.

5. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The application does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), advertising networks, or social media tracking pixels.

6. Sharing Features

The application includes an optional feature that allows you to share your monthly bill summary via WhatsApp or standard text messaging. This action is initiated solely by you using Android's native system share dialog. No data is sent automatically or without your explicit action.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
